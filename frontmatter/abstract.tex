You should follow the \acrfull{imrad} structure for the abstract too. This is what the Guide to Writing a Senior Thesis in Engineering at Harvard University \footnote{https://seas.harvard.edu/media/86521/download} says about the abstract:
\begin{quotation}
    The Abstract should be a single paragraph and should not exceed \textbf{250 words}. It should be designed to define clearly what is dealt with in the thesis and should \textbf{(1)} state the principal objectives and scope of the investigation, \textbf{(2)} describe the methods employed, \textbf{(3)} summarize the results, and \textbf{(4)} state the principal conclusions. Most of the abstract should be written in the \textbf{past tense}, because it refers to completed work. The abstract should never give any information or conclusion that is not stated in the thesis. References to the literature should not be cited in the abstract. Avoid using abbreviations in the abstract.
\end{quotation}
