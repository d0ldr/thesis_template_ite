\chapter{Conclusion\sv{Slutsats}}
\label{chap:conclusion}
\begin{instructions}
Use about half a page to summarize what you have achieved. Did you solve the problem in Section~\ref{sec:problem}? If you did, how did you solve it? If not, why? What methods did you use? Did you fulfill all the requirements? If not, why? What results did you achieve?

Finally, the Conclusion is much like the Abstract, but here you elaborate explicitly on the answers to your problem statement, Section~\ref{sec:problem}. Here, you can also cite other’s work.
\end{instructions}
