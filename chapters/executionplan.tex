\chapter{Execution Plan \fcolorbox{green}{green!10}{(Sv: Genomförandeplan)}}
\label{chap:plan}
\begin{onlymidterm}
This chapter is a refined version of the execution plan presented in the Project Plan. The timetable (Gantt chart) must be updated.

\end{onlymidterm}
Tell the reader how you plan to organise your work. Include a timetable (Gantt chart) showing milestones and planned deadlines that include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item deliverables and deadlines to supervisor ahead of examinations,
    \item deliverables and deadlines to examiner ahead of examinations,
    \item deliverables and deadlines for informing yourselves for the technical background,
    \item deadlines for practical work.
\end{itemize}

\begin{onlymidterm}
\section{Follow-up of the Project Plan \fcolorbox{green}{green!10}{(Sv: Uppföljning av projektplanen)}}
\label{sec:planfollowup}

If the project plan has changed, this should be stated, for example, if you have changed the requirements specification (Section~\ref{sec:req}) or the project boundaries. Explain why the changes were made and how they affect the remaining work.
\end{onlymidterm}
