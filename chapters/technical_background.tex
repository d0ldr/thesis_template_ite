\chapter{Technical Background \fcolorbox{green}{green!10}{(Sv: Teknisk bakgrund)}}
\label{chap:tech_bg}

Introduce the reader to the concepts that are needed to understand the problem and the artifact you will build.
You need to use \textbf{technical language} 
\begin{itemize}
    \item for the concepts that you should know about from the courses you have taken during your education \textbf{and}
    \item for the concepts that are introduced in the literature that you read to better understand the problem \textbf{and}
    \item for the concepts that are introduced in the literature that you read to find how others have solved similar problems.
\end{itemize}
This means that you might have to make definitions. You will find them in books that were used in the courses and in published articles.
